#+DESCRIPTION: Pick what to do today and put the rest where you can find it later.
#+KEYWORDS: daily review organize inbox today schedule planned waiting anytime someday logbook
#+HELP_TOPIC: tasks
#+LANGUAGE: en

* Capture first, then plan
When something comes to mind, put it in Inbox. When you review Inbox, decide what to do next. For something you will do today, schedule it for Today. For a specific day in the future, set its date and look for it in Planned.

* Choose the right list
| List | When to use it |
|-
| Today | See tasks due today, overdue tasks, and tasks in their deadline warning period. |
| Planned | See what is scheduled from tomorrow onward. |
| Waiting | Track work that depends on someone else. |
| Anytime | Find tasks with no scheduled date or deadline. |
| Someday | Keep things you may want to do later. |
| Notes | Keep reference material. |
| Logbook | Revisit completed or canceled tasks. |

For more on scheduled dates and deadlines, see [[file:help-dates.org][Set scheduled dates, deadlines, and reminders]].
